### Desktop Support Analyst at the University of Victoria

My current role is as a Desktop Support Analyst at the University of Victoria, where I work within IT to support faculty, staff, and students across academic and administrative departments.

The work is genuinely varied, which I love. One hour I might be diagnosing a Windows Group Policy issue in an administrative office, and the next I'm in a research lab getting a workstation back online that controls a microscope or an NMR device. That mix of enterprise IT and highly specialized research computing keeps things interesting in a way that a purely software-facing role wouldn't.

Some of what I get up to on a regular basis:

*   Providing tier 1/2 desktop support across Windows, macOS, and Linux environments for departments across the university
*   Device management through Microsoft Intune, Jamf, Active Directory, and Group Policy in a managed desktop environment
*   Supporting lab instrumentation computers used by research faculty and staff, such as devices that drive microscopes, NMR spectrometers, and other precision equipment
*   Contributing to IT infrastructure projects and desktop hardware refresh initiatives
*   Writing and maintaining technical documentation for supported departments
*   Delivering one-on-one and small-group training for users at all levels of technical experience

It's also the first time I've worked in a role where the communication side of the job is genuinely as important as the technical side. Helping someone who is deeply frustrated with their computer requires just as much patience and empathy as it does problem-solving.
